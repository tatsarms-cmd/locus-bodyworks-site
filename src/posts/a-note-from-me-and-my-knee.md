I blew it!

I recently hurt my knee, and it *has* changed how I'll be showing up for the next little while.

What this means for you: class is absolutely still happening after June 11th. I'm feeling confident in my ability to lead through strong verbal cueing and detailed instruction, and I fully plan to continue offering a supportive and intentional experience. That said, I won't be demo-ing as much physically.

I wanted to be upfront about this so there are no surprises when you walk in the room. I deeply value showing up with honesty and transparency, and I trust that the heart of what we do in class goes well beyond physical mimicry. That said, I know this might be a different experience for some of you — and if you're someone who relies heavily on visual demonstration or feel like this changes your decision to attend, please don't hesitate to reach out. If you have a class pass and would rather wait, I'm happy to work something out with you.

Otherwise, I really hope to still see you in class. I'm excited to move together, even if I'm doing a little less moving on my end for now.

With gratitude and a bit of a hobble,
-Tatiana

—

**UPDATE:** Thank you for all your support and patience while I figure out this knee situation :) As of now, this Wednesday, May 21, will be my final class for 2 weeks as I will be going in for surgery. Class will tentatively return June 11th.
